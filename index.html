Madness Uploader

Madness Uploader is the private upload tool behind the Madness YouTube channel (youtube.com/@Madness_BH), which publishes explainers on how computer hardware is made and how to run free tools on your own PC.

The channel owner uses it to upload their own videos, thumbnails and captions to their own channel. It is not a public service and has no other users.
